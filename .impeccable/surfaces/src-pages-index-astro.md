# Surface brief: meal plan viewer (index)

Mode: Operate. One screen, phone-first, used one-handed while eating or assembling a plate.
Job: pick the training time, then read each meal's options and quantities in seconds.
Content: the nutritionist's plan, verbatim numbers. Spanish (Argentina).
Unresolved: hosting, offline use. Decision round skipped by the user's pace; the assigned direction is built as dealt.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The day is a stack of fruit-crate ends from a verdulería. Each meal owns one flat, saturated crate-label colour, so the colour alone tells you which meal you are in. It refuses the grey-card dashboard and the calorie-tracker ring.

OWN-WORLD: Flat unmodulated colour fields on a pale celery ground, ink-green text. Six produce colours: lime, lemon, tomato, tangerine, beet pink, leaf green, grape. One expanded heavy grotesk for headings and numerals, the same family at normal width for reading. No gradients, no texture, no shadows beyond one soft elevation on the open panel.

STORY: The visitor sees the day's order of meals, taps one to see the exact options and quantities, and trusts that the numbers are the nutritionist's. They never need to count or compute.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top: title and a two-option segmented control (Mañana / 19:00) that reorders the stack. Under it a single line with the daily total (1.700 kcal, 141 P, 119 C, 72 G). Then the first crate cards, full width, each a saturated colour field with its sequence number, meal name and kcal. The control sits within thumb reach at the top of a 390 px screen.

FORM: Grounded candidate 3 of 7 (verdulería crate labels), seed: 812b5a3af7edf7ab.
